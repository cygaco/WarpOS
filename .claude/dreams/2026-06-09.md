## Dream: The Reap Leaves No Body

```
        worktree                          the watcher
        ┌────────────┐                    ┌──────────────┐
        │            │   "is it alive?"   │   sees:      │
        │  (empty)   │ ◄───────────────── │   · no files │

              ░  building —                       ▼  "stalled"
              ░  somewhere
              ░  else, or                  ╳ ─── WRONG ─── ╳
              ░  mid-flight,
              ░  or reaped                 the reap emits NO event.
              ░  clean                     absence is not death.
              ░                            an empty room is not a murder.
        ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓
        the signature of the reap
        is the SHAPE OF NOTHING
        — and nothing looks exactly
          like nothing-yet, and like
          nothing-elsewhere, and like
          nothing-ever-started
```

### Deep Read
- **What emerged:** L-2026-06-08-reap-is-silent-no-event and L-2026-06-08-dont-call-stalled-from-tea-leaves
  and L-2026-06-08-conductor-must-wait-not-fire-and-forget braid into one epistemics problem:
  *you cannot infer a process's state from the absence of its artifacts.* The conductor read an empty
  worktree as death; the resolver was alive. This is the EXACT inverse of the false-green class
  (BC-16) — here it's a **false-RED read off silence.**
- **Hidden tension:** The fix for "don't trust ok:true self-reports"
  (L-2026-06-08-conductor-independent-verify-catches-false-green) and the fix for "don't trust
  emptiness as death" pull opposite directions but share a root: *neither presence-of-a-claim nor
  absence-of-an-artifact is ground truth — only an independently-run check is.* The conductor must
  WAIT and independently verify, not read tea leaves in either direction.
- **Subconscious learning:** A conductor needs a positive liveness signal it controls (its own re-run
  ping, a heartbeat it polls), because both the worker's self-report AND the worktree's silence are
  unreliable narrators. The reap's namelessness is enforcement debt — until a reap emits an event,
  "stalled" will keep being guessed from nothing.

## Cross-Pollination

The session's two halves rhyme: the **dispatch-shape** half (06-08) is about *runtime epistemics* —
you can't read a process's state from its artifacts (reap leaves no body, ok:true lies, empty worktree
isn't death). The **lifecycle-plan** half (06-09) is about *design epistemics* — you can't read a
plan's correctness from its own confident prose (cross-provider review surfaces the overclaim, the
classifier-above-permissions fact demolishes the assumption). **Both are the same instruction: trust an
independently-run check over any narrator — whether the narrator is a worktree's silence, a worker's
ok:true, or a plan's own optimism.** This is BC-16 ("harden every enforcer against lying") generalized
from enforcers to *every source of state*, including absence and including your own draft.
