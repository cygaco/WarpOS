### Deep Read
- **What emerged:** The whole session was a watcher *almost unable to see its own blind spot*. I built a gate to catch "things shipped incomplete," and the gate itself shipped incomplete (verifyTyped wired to nothing). The lantern being unlit until three cross-provider strangers shouted is the literal shape of why the gauntlet is non-negotiable on enforcer work — a self-checking thing cannot be trusted to check itself.
- **Hidden tension:** Detector vs. cure. The box (sealed capsule) proves the kit *can* stand alone — but the "100 missing templates" turned out to be present-with-wrong-labels. I almost sent the next session to *author* what already exists. The dream's empty room is honest; the workshop's map (manifest pointers) is not. The disease isn't absence — it's mislabeling. Building alarms is easier than fixing addresses.
- **Subconscious learning:** When a builder goes silent, stop waiting and pick up the hammer — but notice the deeper move: I trusted *direct work over delegation* precisely when the delegation channel was unreliable. The reusable lesson isn't "always foreground"; it's "match the tool to the channel's reliability, and make the channel's failure self-announcing" (ED-018). The lantern must light itself, or someone must be sworn to shout.
