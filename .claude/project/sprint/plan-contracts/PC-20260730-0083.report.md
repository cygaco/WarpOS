## Interpreted Intent

Move an existing, working audit capability into a new host rather than rewriting it, and settle the readiness-number question permanently while doing so. The word 'converge' in the epic's framing is a trap: this is an ADOPTION of score.js plus a deliberate NON-port of the checklist proxy, not a reconciliation of two implementations. Secondary intent: produce the real dogfood data that lets the receipt schema be typed at all, since the engine deliberately emits an untyped interior until this sprint fills it.

## Desired Outcome

A founder points Vlad at a repository and gets a readiness assessment with a receipt — one number, one source of truth, and an honest 'NOT SCORED' where a dimension cannot be derived rather than a fabricated score.
