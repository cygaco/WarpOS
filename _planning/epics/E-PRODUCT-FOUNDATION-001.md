## 4. Acceptance criteria
- A founder can scaffold a product, see 'here is what we are using, here is where you sign up and what I need' (founders checklist + declared stack), defer all human-only work, and the product remains ready-to-go — no AI-side work blocks on the human's clock.
- A product that launches via this on-ramp can compute activation/retention (D1/D7) from day zero without any lastmile analytics work.
- A founder can see who signed up and unblock/comp a user from /admin without touching code or a database console.
- Every net-new contract in this epic names its enforcer at plan-time (β constraint 1) — no aspirational tracks.
- BaaS choices remain guide RECOMMENDATIONS, not committed dependencies, preserving the operator's 'until I learn more' provisionality.
